**The real takeaway on interpreting adaptive scores:** raw accuracy
across the two sections of one measure isn't directly comparable, since
section 2's difficulty depends on section 1's result. Quant "dropping"
from 92% to 60% looks like a decline read naively; read correctly, it's
evidence the algorithm pushed you into harder territory *because* section
1 went well — which is the outcome you want.
