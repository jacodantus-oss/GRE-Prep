# Exam Day — August 22, 2026

Not a study day. Logistics only. Read this once, the day before, and be done
with it.

## Bring

- **Valid, original, unexpired photo ID** (passport, driver's license, or
  national ID). First and last name must **exactly match your registration**
  — mismatches mean you don't get to test, no refund. Photocopies and phone
  photos of your ID do not count.
- Your registration confirmation.
- Nothing else goes into the testing room — **only your ID and a mask** (if
  you choose to wear one) are permitted with you. No phone, no watch, no
  scratch paper of your own (scratch paper/booklet is provided at the
  center).

## Timing

- **Arrive at least 30 minutes early.** Late arrival can mean you're turned
  away with the fee forfeited.
- Confirm your test center's exact address and parking/transit situation the
  night before — not test-day morning.

## The night before / morning of

- No new content. Day 18 was the last practice.
- Normal sleep, normal breakfast — nothing new or different from your
  routine on any other exam day.
- Reread your AWA Issue essay template once so it's fresh, then stop.

## During the test

- Optional short breaks are built into the GRE between sections — use them:
  stand up, drink water, reset. Don't skip them to "save time," pacing
  discipline this whole course was built around applies to the break
  structure too.
- If a section goes badly, let it go. The GRE's section-adaptive scoring
  means dwelling on a rough section costs you more than the section itself
  did.
