# Day 11: RC Timed Practice & Mixed Short Verbal Drilling

**Date:** August 14, 2026 · **Time budget:** ~2 hours

Two straight 8/8s on self-generated RC passages, against 57% on Kaplan's
real test, means the homemade material has been undershooting true GRE
difficulty. Today's RC practice comes from real external sources instead —
an honest test of whether passage mapping holds up on the real thing. Also:
**Sentence Equivalence is back in its real format today** — genuine 6-word,
pick-exactly-2 questions, not the pre-built pair-choices from Day 5. The
site's quiz widget now supports it properly.

## Hour 1 — RC timed practice (real material)

Use ETS's own official sample questions: **[ETS — Sample Questions on the GRE General Test](https://www.ets.org/pdfs/gre/sample-question-overview.pdf)**
(PDF). Work through the Verbal Reasoning section — Reading Comprehension,
Text Completion, and Sentence Equivalence samples — timed at real GRE pace
(roughly 1-1.5 min per question). This is as close to true exam difficulty
and style as free material gets, since ETS wrote it.

**Backup / extra volume:** [Magoosh — 22 GRE Verbal Practice Questions with Explanations](https://magoosh.com/gre/gre-verbal-practice-questions/)
if you want more after the ETS set, or if that PDF is inaccessible for any
reason.

Apply Day 10's strategy directly: map each passage's structure before
answering, use the question-type table to pick your approach, and name the
trap type on anything you have to think hard about or get wrong.
