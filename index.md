## How this works

1. **Syllabus** — 18 days, sequenced by diagnostic priority: Quant arithmetic accuracy → Verbal vocab/strategy → pacing discipline → AWA structure → Reading Comp, with three full-length checkpoints.
2. **Per day**: quick concept/rule review → strategy (curated resource + takeaways) → timed practice → instant-graded check → log. Day 4+ adds a second hour: daily vocab, recurring RC reps, and a cumulative timed drill at real section pace.
3. **Submissions get graded here, live** — no automated Action; feedback lands the same session you submit.
4. **Three checkpoints**: a clean Reading Comp re-diagnostic early on (the baseline RC score was depressed by a test-platform glitch), a second full-length diagnostic (free third-party test) on Aug 12 to calibrate pacing mid-course, and the real PowerPrep dress rehearsal on Aug 18.
